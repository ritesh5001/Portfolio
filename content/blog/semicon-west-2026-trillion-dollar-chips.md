**SEMICON West 2026, the chip industry's biggest North American event, runs October 13–15 at the Moscone Center in San Francisco, framed around one milestone: the semiconductor industry passing $1 trillion in annual revenue,** which SEMI says has happened almost four years ahead of earlier projections.

## What's on the agenda

Under the theme "Transform Tomorrow", the program covers the road from $1 trillion to $2 trillion:

- **Advanced manufacturing:** smaller nodes, new packaging and smarter factories.
- **AI and next-generation computing:** the demand driver behind almost every number this year.
- **Supply chain resilience:** spreading production across more countries, including India.
- **Sustainability and workforce:** power, water, emissions, and the shortage of trained engineers.

There are CEO keynotes plus focused forums on design, cybersecurity, smart manufacturing, smart mobility and sustainability.

## Why $1 trillion matters beyond chips

Chips used to be a cyclical business tied to PCs and phones. Hitting $1 trillion years early tells you AI has changed that: data centres are now the biggest buyer. For software, that has three effects:

1. **AI becomes a utility.** As capacity grows, AI APIs get cheaper and faster, the same way cloud hosting did a decade ago.
2. **Every software product gets an AI layer.** Customers will expect search that understands them, support that answers instantly, and content that adapts.
3. **India is in the story.** With fabs and packaging plants coming up in Gujarat and Assam, India is becoming part of the hardware supply chain, not just the software one.
