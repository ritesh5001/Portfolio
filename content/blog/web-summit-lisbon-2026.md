**Web Summit 2026 takes place November 9–12 in Lisbon, with more than 70,000 attendees expected.** The 2025 edition drew about 71,000 people from 157 countries. It includes an AI Summit track, with AI and machine learning among the most-represented areas. It's one of the world's largest tech gatherings of startups, investors, corporates and media in one place.

## What happens at Web Summit

- **Startup exhibition:** thousands of startups across Alpha, Beta and Growth programmes.
- **Investor meetings** through the event app and side events.
- **Talks** across AI, fintech, climate, media and more.
- **Night events** where many of the real conversations happen.

Check websummit.com for official speakers, venues and ticket details.

## Should an Indian startup go?

**Worth it if:** you're raising from European or global investors, selling to European customers, or have a product ready for international press.

**Probably not yet if:** you're pre-product, focused only on India, or the trip would eat most of your runway.

## Turning a big event into results

1. **Book meetings before you arrive** using the event app. Walking the floor alone rarely works.
2. **Have a 30-second story** and a live demo on your phone.
3. **Follow up within 48 hours,** while people still remember you.
4. **Make sure your website does the selling.** Everyone you meet will check it. A fast site with a clear value proposition, case studies, pricing or a demo link, and a way to book a call converts curiosity into meetings.

Many founders spend heavily on the trip and neglect the website people visit afterwards.
