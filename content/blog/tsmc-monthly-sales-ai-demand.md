**TSMC reported September 2026 revenue of NT$511.86 billion, up 54.6% from a year earlier and just 0.6% below August's record of NT$514.81 billion.** Third-quarter revenue hit a record NT$1.494 trillion, beating the top of TSMC's own forecast, and revenue for the first nine months is up 41.1%. Because TSMC makes the vast majority of the world's advanced AI chips, these monthly numbers are one of the clearest real-time signals of AI demand.

## Why TSMC's monthly sales matter

Most companies report quarterly. TSMC publishes revenue every month, around the 10th. Since it manufactures chips for Nvidia, AMD, Apple, Broadcom and the big cloud providers' in-house designs, its revenue is a rolling, near-real-time read on how much AI hardware is actually being built.

## What the numbers show, and don't

- **Growth is strong and broad:** 54.6% year-on-year is exceptional for a company of TSMC's size.
- **Momentum is steady:** September roughly matched August's record, with no sign of a sudden drop.
- **The monthly release doesn't split out AI.** It gives no customers, nodes or chip types. High-performance computing, which includes AI, is reported elsewhere as around two-thirds of revenue, but that isn't tied to the September figure.

Some coverage called September a record month, but August set the monthly record. The quarter was the record.

## What it means for businesses building with AI

1. **Capacity keeps growing**, backed by TSMC's record capital spending plans.
2. **AI costs keep falling** as that capacity comes online.
3. **AI features are becoming standard**, so customers will expect smarter search and support from businesses of every size.
