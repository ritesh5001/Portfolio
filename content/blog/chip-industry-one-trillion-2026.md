**Global semiconductor sales have passed $1 trillion for the first time, reaching that total in just the first eight months of 2026, according to the Semiconductor Industry Association's October 5 release.** August alone brought in $159.7 billion, and the SIA now projects sales above $1.6 trillion for the full year. For context, 2025 sales were $791.7 billion, and in February the SIA was saying 2026 *might* reach about $1 trillion.

## What's driving it

- **AI data centres:** GPUs, custom AI accelerators, high-bandwidth memory and networking chips.
- **Memory prices:** the [HBM and DRAM shortage](/blog/hbm-memory-shortage) has pushed prices sharply higher, inflating revenue.
- **Broad recovery** in PCs, phones and industrial chips, though AI dominates the growth.

Not everyone is bullish. Some analysts warn that memory price spikes and AI spending could reverse sharply and point to a coming downturn. Booms in this industry have always been cyclical.

## Why a trillion-dollar chip industry matters to software

1. **AI is infrastructure now.** Spending at this scale means AI capabilities will keep improving and keep getting cheaper per request.
2. **Every business will be expected to use AI** in search, support and content, the way every business was expected to have a mobile site a decade ago.
3. **India's role is growing,** with packaging plants in [Sanand](/blog/india-osat-sanand-chip-cluster) and Tata's [Dholera fab](/blog/tata-asml-dholera-fab) in progress.
