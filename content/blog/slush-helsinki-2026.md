**Slush 2026 takes place November 18–19 at Messukeskus in Helsinki.** It's one of the world's most founder- and investor-focused startup events: the organisers cite 12,000+ attendees, 25,000+ meetings, 600+ side events, and roughly one VC for every two founders. Unlike broader tech conferences, Slush is built around getting founders in front of investors.

## What makes Slush different

- **Investor density:** a high ratio of active investors to founders.
- **Matchmaking:** double opt-in meetings booked through the event's tool before and during the event.
- **Slush 100:** the startup pitch competition, whose winner typically receives an equity investment from a syndicate of VCs.
- **Side events** across Helsinki throughout the week.

Ticket prices and passes vary by type (startup, investor, single-day), so check slush.org for current details.

## Should an Indian founder go?

**Worth it if:** you're raising a seed or Series A round from European or global investors, or expanding into the Nordics and Europe.

**Maybe not yet if:** you have no product traction, or your market and investors are entirely in India.

## How to prepare

1. **Book meetings early** through the matchmaking tool, and research each investor's portfolio first.
2. **Bring traction, not just vision:** users, revenue, growth and retention.
3. **Demo on your phone,** and make sure it works offline.
4. **Have a crisp one-pager** and data room ready.
5. **Fix your website before you fly.** Every investor you meet will look you up. A fast, clear site with your product, traction, team and a way to book a follow-up does the selling while you're on the next call.

Many founders spend weeks on the deck and ignore the website investors check right after the meeting.
