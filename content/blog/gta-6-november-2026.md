**Grand Theft Auto VI is set to launch on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S** (a PC version hasn't been announced). It's one of the most anticipated entertainment launches ever, after delays from 2025 to May 2026 and then to November. Launches this big routinely strain stores, download servers and websites. The same thing, on a smaller scale, happens to ordinary businesses on sale days, festival launches and viral moments.

## What goes wrong on launch day

- **The homepage goes down** under a burst of traffic it was never tested for.
- **Checkout fails** because the database or payment integration can't keep up.
- **Inventory oversells** when many people buy the last units at the same moment.
- **Third-party scripts** (chat widgets, analytics, popups) slow every page to a crawl.

## How to make your site survive its big day

1. **Serve pages from a CDN.** Static and cached pages scale almost infinitely. This is a big reason I build on server-rendered frameworks with caching.
2. **Load-test before the event** at several times your expected peak.
3. **Protect checkout:** queue requests if needed, use atomic stock updates so you don't oversell, and confirm your payment gateway's rate limits.
4. **Strip non-essential scripts** for the sale period.
5. **Pre-warm caches** and avoid deploying new code on launch day.
6. **Watch it live:** uptime, error rates and checkout success, with someone ready to act.
7. **Have a fallback page:** a fast, static "high demand, please wait" page beats an error.

For Indian stores, Diwali sales, Big Billion-style campaigns and influencer shout-outs create exactly these spikes.
