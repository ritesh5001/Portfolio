**Nvidia is expected to report its fiscal third-quarter 2027 results around November 18, 2026, after guiding to revenue of about $108 billion (plus or minus 2%) and a gross margin around 74%.** The guidance assumes zero data-centre compute revenue from China. Last quarter it reported $96.2 billion, up 106% year on year, and its CFO said the company expects around 70% revenue growth in fiscal 2028. Confirm the exact date on Nvidia's investor relations site.

## What to watch in the report

- **Did it beat $108 billion,** and by how much? Recent quarters have beaten guidance, and markets expect it.
- **Next quarter's guidance:** the signal for AI spending heading into 2027.
- **Data-centre mix:** compute versus networking, and how fast the newest platforms are ramping.
- **Gross margin:** whether new-product ramps pressure the ~74% target.
- **Supply constraints:** memory ([HBM is sold out](/blog/hbm-memory-shortage)), packaging capacity and power availability.
- **Customer concentration** and how much the big clouds and AI labs keep spending, as more of them build [their own chips](/blog/companies-building-own-ai-chips).

## Why AI builders should care

Nvidia's results are a proxy for the whole AI build-out. Strong numbers mean more capacity coming online, which over time means cheaper, faster AI APIs for everyone. A slowdown would hint at a pause in that curve.
