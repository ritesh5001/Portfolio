For most businesses in Delhi NCR, a website has one job: **turn a local search into a phone call, a WhatsApp message or a booking.** A coaching institute in Laxmi Nagar, a clinic in South Delhi and a showroom in Karol Bagh don't need an elaborate site. They need one that loads instantly on a phone, shows up when someone searches nearby, and makes it effortless to get in touch.

Here's what I'd build for a Delhi business, and what I learned building one for a Delhi service business.

## What Delhi businesses need from a website

**Service businesses** (clinics, salons, trainers, repair services, consultants) win or lose on enquiries. The site should put the phone number and WhatsApp button where a thumb can reach them, show prices or price ranges where you can, and have a short booking or callback form that asks for no more than it needs.

**Coaching and training institutes** need course pages that rank on their own ("SSC coaching in Laxmi Nagar", "IELTS classes in Rohini"), batch timings, fee details, results and testimonials, plus a demo-class or counselling booking.

**Retailers and wholesalers** in markets like Chandni Chowk, Karol Bagh and Lajpat Nagar increasingly sell beyond walk-in customers. A catalogue site with WhatsApp ordering is often the right first step before a full online store.

**Corporates and startups in Gurugram and Noida** usually need something different: a fast company site plus custom software, such as internal dashboards, client portals or AI features. That's covered in my [Bengaluru guide](/blog/website-development-in-bangalore), and the approach is the same.

## A Delhi example: Lady Scooty Trainer

[Lady Scooty Trainer](/work) teaches women to ride scooters in Delhi NCR with female trainers, at or near the learner's home. The site's job is narrow and clear: build trust (women-only trainers, thousands of learners trained) and get a consultation booked. So the page leads with that promise, offers a **Book Your Free Consultation** button and a click-to-call number above the fold, keeps WhatsApp one tap away throughout, and gives corporate and CSR training its own section because that's a separate buyer.

That pattern of one clear promise, one main action and trust on the first screen works for almost any Delhi service business.

## Getting found in local search

Ranking for "near me" searches depends on more than the website:

- **A Google Business Profile** with the right category, service area, photos, and reviews you actively ask for.
- **A page per service, and per area if you genuinely serve it**, rather than one page that lists everything. "Physiotherapy in Dwarka" and "physiotherapy home visits in Delhi" are different searches.
- **Consistent name, address and phone number** everywhere you're listed: Google, Justdial, IndiaMART, social profiles.
- **A fast, server-rendered site.** If your pages are blank until JavaScript loads, search engines see less of them. ([Here's how that bit me](/blog/react-seo-google-saw-empty-page).)
