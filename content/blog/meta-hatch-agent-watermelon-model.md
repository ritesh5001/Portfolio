**Meta has not announced Hatch or Watermelon. Both come from reporting on internal documents, mainly by The Information:** Hatch is described as a consumer AI agent that can act on your behalf across apps, and Watermelon as a new flagship model targeted for October 2026. Here's what's been reported, and why small businesses should pay attention even before anything ships.

## What's been reported

- **Hatch** is described as a consumer version of Meta's internal agent technology. Reported integrations include DoorDash, Etsy, Reddit, Yelp and Outlook, so it could order food, find a service or manage email for the user.
- **Pricing:** Meta is reportedly weighing a premium tier of up to $199.99 a month. That's a consideration, not an announced price.
- **Watermelon** is reportedly targeted for October. Internal claims put it at parity with a leading OpenAI model on Meta's own benchmarks, which nobody outside Meta has verified.

## What's still unknown

The launch date, the price, which countries get it, and whether Watermelon ships as part of Meta's existing model family or on its own. A codename and a target month aren't a release.

## Why this matters for businesses in India

Meta owns WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook: the places Indian customers already talk to businesses. If an agent like Hatch can take actions *for* customers, the way customers find and buy from you starts to change.

- **Agents read your website, not your Instagram reels.** An agent booking a table or comparing products needs clean, structured information: prices, availability, timings, policies. A fast site with proper structured data is easier for an agent to use, and an agent that can't use your site will use your competitor's.
- **WhatsApp ordering gets more automated.** Expect more customer conversations to start with an AI assistant on the customer's side. Clear catalogues and quick replies matter more, not less.
- **Your site needs to work without JavaScript tricks.** Server-rendered pages are easier for both search engines and AI agents to read.
