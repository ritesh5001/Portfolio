Many of Kolkata's best businesses are decades old and built on reputation: handloom saree houses, sweet shops, wholesalers in Burrabazar, bookshops on College Street. **Taking a business like that online isn't about looking modern. It's about letting customers outside the neighbourhood buy what loyal customers already trust**, with a catalogue that does the product justice and a checkout that works for everyone.

## What a traditional Kolkata business needs online

**A catalogue that matches how the product is sold.** Bengal handloom (tant, jamdani, Baluchari) is bought on weave, fabric, colour and occasion, so the store should let people filter by exactly those things. A generic "sarees" category with 400 products in it loses buyers.

**Photos that show the craft.** Drape shots, close-ups of the weave and the border, and the fall of the fabric. For handloom, the photography sells more than the copy does.

**Checkout for every kind of buyer.** UPI for younger shoppers, COD for first-time buyers, and international cards if you ship abroad. Bengali families outside India are a real market for handloom and festival wear.

**WhatsApp ordering.** Many family businesses already sell this way. The website should make it easier: share a product link straight into WhatsApp, and get order notifications there.

**Seasonal collections.** Durga Puja, Poila Baisakh and wedding season drive most of the year's sales. The site needs collection pages you can set up quickly and feature on the homepage, without calling a developer each time.

## Sweets and food businesses

Food is harder online than sarees because of shelf life and delivery windows. What works: a small, well-photographed range of items that travel well (dry sweets, gift boxes, packaged snacks); clear dispatch days and delivery estimates; pincode checks that block areas you can't reach in time; and gift-box ordering for festivals and corporate orders.

## Bengali and English

If your customers search in Bengali, the site can carry Bengali product names and pages alongside English. AI makes the first draft of translations fast, but a native speaker should review anything customers will read. Machine-translated product copy shows.

## Questions Kolkata businesses ask

### We already sell on WhatsApp. Why build a website?
A website lets people outside your network find you through Google, browse without messaging, and pay up front. WhatsApp stays your support and repeat-order channel.

### Can we sell internationally?
Yes, with international payments, shipping rates by country and prices that make sense abroad. Customs and courier choice need planning first.
