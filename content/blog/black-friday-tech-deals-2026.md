**Black Friday falls on Friday, November 27, 2026, followed by Cyber Monday on November 30.** For Indian stores it's become a second big sale moment after the festive season, and for anyone selling abroad it's the biggest weekend of the year. Two weeks is enough time to make sure your store is fast, stable and ready to convert, if you start now.

## The two-week checklist

**Speed and stability**
1. **Test your store on a mid-range Android phone over 4G.** If the homepage takes more than three seconds, fix images and remove heavy scripts first. See [Core Web Vitals](/blog/core-web-vitals-seo).
2. **Load-test checkout** at several times your normal peak, and confirm your hosting plan can handle it.
3. **Turn on page caching and a CDN.**
4. **Freeze changes:** no plugin updates, theme changes or new apps during sale week.
5. **Back up everything** the day before.

**Checkout and payments**
6. **Test every payment method:** UPI, cards, net banking, wallets, COD and international cards.
7. **Check stock logic** so you don't oversell limited items.
8. **Set up abandoned-cart reminders** by email and WhatsApp.

**Offers and pages**
9. **Build sale pages early** and get them indexed: "Black Friday deals on [your category]".
10. **Update product structured data** with sale prices so Google shows them.
11. **Make offers simple:** one clear discount beats complicated stacking rules.

**Support and operations**
12. **Prepare canned replies** for shipping times, returns and COD.
13. **Brief your courier partner** on expected volume.

The lessons from huge launches apply here too. I covered them in [what big launch days teach about web traffic](/blog/gta-6-november-2026).
