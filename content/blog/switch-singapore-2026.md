**SWITCH, the Singapore Week of Innovation and Technology, runs October 27–29, 2026 at Marina Bay Sands.** It's one of Southeast Asia's flagship startup events, government-backed and built around deep-tech startups, corporate innovation teams and investors. Dealroom puts attendance at around 25,000, and the 2025 edition reportedly drew about 28,000 visitors and 400 exhibitors.

## What happens at SWITCH

- **Keynotes and showcases** on deep tech, AI, sustainability and fintech.
- **Corporate innovation programmes** where large companies look for startup partners.
- **SLINGSHOT**, the startup pitch competition, listed with over S$2.2 million in grant prizes for 2026.
- **Matching zones**, including Enterprise Singapore's Global Innovation Alliance programmes.

There's a free attendee pass for keynotes and showcases, and paid all-access passes. Check the official site, switchsg.org, for current pricing.

## Why Indian founders should pay attention

Singapore is a common base for Indian startups expanding into Southeast Asia: similar time zones, English as the business language, and an investor community that knows the Indian market. Events like SWITCH compress months of introductions into three days.

## Making a trip count

1. **Book meetings before you fly.** Use the event's matching tools to line up investors and corporates in advance.
2. **Have a product, not just slides.** A working demo on your phone beats a deck.
3. **Make your website do the follow-up.** Everyone you meet will look you up within a day. A fast site with a clear product story, case studies and a way to book a demo turns introductions into conversations.

That last point is where a lot of founders leak momentum.
