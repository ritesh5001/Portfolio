**Terafab is a planned chip manufacturing complex in Grimes County, Texas, backed by SpaceX and Tesla, meant to make the chips behind Optimus robots, Cybercab robotaxis and SpaceX's planned space-based data centres.** The latest reporting, from August 2026, puts the initial investment at $16.8 billion for a site of over 100 million square feet employing at least 3,000 people, covering manufacturing, packaging and testing of advanced logic and memory chips.

## Why the cost figures keep changing

Reported numbers have moved a lot:

- **March 2026 announcement:** at least $20 billion.
- **May 2026 public notice:** an initial $55 billion, rising to as much as $119 billion if every phase is built.
- **August 2026 report:** an initial $16.8 billion.

These probably describe different phases and scopes rather than contradictions, but treat any single figure with caution until the companies publish firm plans.

## What it's for

SpaceX cites demand for more than a terawatt of compute across robots, vehicles and orbital data centres. Earlier reporting described separate fabs, each dedicated to a single chip design: a vertically integrated approach that avoids competing for TSMC's capacity.

## Why it matters

1. **More AI chip capacity** in the world, eventually.
2. **Vertical integration is spreading.** Large AI users increasingly want their own silicon instead of competing for the same foundry capacity.
3. **Execution risk is real.** Building a leading-edge fab is extremely hard, even for well-funded newcomers. Timelines often slip.
