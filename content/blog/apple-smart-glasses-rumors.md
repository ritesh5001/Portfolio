**Apple's first smart glasses, reportedly codenamed N50, have slipped to a launch around the end of 2027, according to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, which could push wider availability into 2028.** Earlier reports had pointed to a late-2026 reveal. Nothing is confirmed by Apple, and the timeline has shifted several times.

## What's rumoured

- **Camera glasses first, not full AR:** earlier reports described cameras and audio with Siri and gestures, but no display. Think Meta's Ray-Ban glasses, rather than a Vision Pro on your face.
- **Fashion focus:** multiple frame styles and colours, and oval-shaped cameras.
- **Longer-term ambitions** include health features and eventually true AR.
- **Mac-tethered AR glasses were reportedly cancelled** earlier in 2026, while standalone AR remains a long-term goal.

## Why the delay matters

Meta has a head start with its smart glasses, and Google and others are pushing Android XR. Apple typically arrives later with a more polished product, but each delay gives competitors time to shape what people expect from the category.

## What smart glasses mean for apps and websites

1. **Voice and visual queries grow:** "What is this?" and "Where can I buy this?" asked through glasses. Your product information needs to be findable and structured.
2. **Local businesses benefit** from complete, accurate Google Business Profiles and structured data, since glasses answer "near me" questions.
3. **Short, glanceable content wins:** clear answers, not long pages.
4. **Assistant-driven actions:** booking, ordering and directions triggered by voice.
