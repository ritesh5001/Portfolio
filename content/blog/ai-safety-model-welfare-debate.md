**Anthropic has updated Claude's usage policy, effective November 12, 2026, to bar sustained, needless cruelty toward its AI models,** with Claude's ability to end abusive conversations as the main enforcement tool rather than account bans. That has reopened one of AI's strangest debates: whether AI models could ever have experiences that matter morally (so-called "model welfare"), and whether it's even sensible to ask.

## Where the debate stands

- **Anthropic** started a model welfare research programme in 2025, saying there's no scientific consensus on AI consciousness and that it wants to take the question seriously under uncertainty. Its CEO, Dario Amodei, has said the company doesn't know whether models are conscious but is open to the idea.
- **Critics**, including Microsoft AI's chief Mustafa Suleyman, argue that studying or emphasising AI consciousness is premature and potentially harmful, because it encourages people to treat software as a person.
- **Most researchers** agree today's models aren't known to be conscious. The disagreement is about how to act under uncertainty.

Coverage notes that the policy change itself doesn't explicitly mention model welfare. Anthropic frames it narrowly, around extreme, repeated cruelty with no legitimate purpose.

## How this fits into AI safety

Model welfare is a small, contested corner of a much bigger field. Most AI safety work is about making systems **reliable, honest and controllable**: preventing harmful outputs, resisting manipulation like prompt injection, keeping humans in oversight, and evaluating dangerous capabilities before release.

## What it means for businesses using AI

Very little changes in practice. Normal business use isn't affected. What does matter for your AI features:

1. **Clear usage policies** for your own chatbots and assistants.
2. **Abuse handling:** let assistants end or escalate abusive conversations, which protects your staff and your brand too.
3. **Human oversight** of consequential decisions.
4. **Honest disclosure** that users are talking to an AI.
