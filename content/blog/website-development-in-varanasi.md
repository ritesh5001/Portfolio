Two things bring the world to Varanasi: **Banarasi silk, which carries a geographical indication (GI) tag, and the city itself — the ghats, the Ganga aarti, Kashi Vishwanath and nearby Sarnath.** Weavers and saree houses want buyers across India and abroad, and guesthouses and guides want the visitors who are planning a trip. Both are better served by a website they own than by marketplaces and booking platforms that take a cut.

## Selling Banarasi sarees online

A Banarasi saree is a considered purchase, often for a wedding, and the buyer is spending real money on something they can't touch. The store has to close that gap:

- **Filter by what buyers actually search for**: Katan silk, Organza, Georgette, Tussar; zari type; motifs (butta, jaal, kadhua); occasion; price. A buyer looking for "pure Katan silk Banarasi with kadhua butta" should find it in two clicks.
- **Photography that proves the weave**: the full drape, the pallu, the border, and close-ups of the zari and motifs, ideally with a short video of the fabric in motion.
- **Authenticity front and centre**: handloom or powerloom stated honestly, silk mark or GI details where they apply, and the weaver's story. Authenticity is what separates you from cheap imitations listed under the same name.
- **Checkout built for high-value orders**: UPI and cards, EMI options, COD with an advance for first-time buyers, insured shipping, and clear return terms.

## Guesthouses, boat rides and spiritual tours

Visitors to Varanasi plan carefully, especially from abroad: where to stay near the ghats, how to see the morning and evening aarti, which rituals they can attend. A website that answers those questions and takes a booking wins them before a booking platform does.

- **Room or tour pages with real photos** and exact location: which ghat, how far from the main sites.
- **Bookable experiences**: sunrise boat rides, aarti viewing, Sarnath day trips, puja arrangements.
- **Multilingual pages** for your main visitor countries, properly translated.
- **An AI itinerary planner** that suggests a two- or three-day plan from a visitor's dates. I built one for [Explore Fusion](https://explorefusion.online) with the Groq AI SDK.

## Questions Varanasi businesses ask

### Can we sell to customers abroad?
Yes, with international payments, shipping by country and prices that make sense abroad. Many Banarasi buyers are Indian families overseas shopping for weddings.

### Should we sell on our own website or on marketplaces?
Both, ideally. Marketplaces bring discovery, while your own site keeps the margin and the customer relationship, especially for repeat and referral buyers.

### Can you list hundreds of sarees with variants?
Yes. The catalogue is structured so adding a saree means filling in a form: fabric, zari, motif, colour, price and photos.
