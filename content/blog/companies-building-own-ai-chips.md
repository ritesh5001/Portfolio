**Almost every major AI company is now designing its own AI chips instead of relying only on Nvidia GPUs.** Google has its TPUs, Amazon has Trainium and Inferentia, Microsoft has Maia, and Meta has MTIA. Broadcom says its custom AI chip customers include Google, Meta, Anthropic and OpenAI. And companies like Tesla are going further, planning their own chip production.

## Why they're doing it

- **Cost:** at their scale, even a modest gain in performance per dollar saves billions.
- **Supply security:** less dependence on one vendor during shortages.
- **Workload fit:** chips designed around their own models and serving patterns.
- **Bargaining power:** a credible alternative improves terms with every supplier.

Designing a chip is still extremely hard and expensive. That's why most companies partner with specialists like [Broadcom](/blog/broadcom-custom-ai-chips) rather than doing everything in-house, and why Nvidia's software ecosystem remains a powerful advantage.

## What it means for developers and businesses

1. **Cheaper inference over time** as competition grows beneath the APIs.
2. **The same model may cost and perform differently** depending on which cloud and chip serves it.
3. **Abstraction pays off:** if your app talks to models through one interface, you can move to whichever provider offers the best price and speed.
4. **Cloud choice matters:** each cloud's custom chips favour different models and pricing.

## The bottom line

You'll probably never choose a chip directly. You'll choose a model and a provider, and the chip race underneath makes those choices cheaper and more varied every year.
