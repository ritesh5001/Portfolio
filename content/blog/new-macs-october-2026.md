**Reporting, mostly from Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, points to Apple launching a 14-inch MacBook Pro with the M6 chip and refreshed M6 iMacs around late October 2026,** alongside macOS 27.1. Apple hasn't announced anything, so treat details as rumours until it does.

## What's reported

- **M6 14-inch MacBook Pro and M6 iMacs**, expected to be largely chip upgrades rather than redesigns.
- **No M6 Pro or M6 Max** is planned, per the reports, which makes this a shorter chip cycle than usual.
- **Price** is expected to stay where the current model starts. That's a projection, not a confirmed price.
- **The bigger redesign comes later:** touchscreen OLED high-end models are reported for late 2026 to early 2027, and an M7 generation in the first half of 2027.

## Should a developer buy now or wait?

**Wait a few weeks** if you're buying a base MacBook Pro or iMac anyway. Either you get the M6, or the M5 model gets cheaper.

**Don't wait** if your current machine is slowing you down every day. Months of lost time cost more than the upgrade difference.

**Consider the bigger picture** if you want a Pro or Max chip. The reports suggest those land with the redesign, not this cycle.

## What actually matters for web and AI development

From years of building on Macs, here's what makes a difference to day-to-day development speed:

1. **Memory first.** 16 GB is the minimum for comfortable web development. 32 GB if you run Docker, several dev servers, or local AI models.
2. **Storage.** 512 GB fills fast with `node_modules`, Docker images and models.
3. **Chip generation** matters less than either for typical web work. Any recent M-series chip is fast.

For running local language models, unified memory size matters far more than the chip number.
