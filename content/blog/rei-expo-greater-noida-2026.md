**Renewable Energy India Expo (REI Expo) 2026 runs October 22–24 at India Expo Mart in Greater Noida, Delhi NCR,** covering solar, bio-energy, energy storage, green hydrogen and e-mobility. The organiser describes it as one of Asia's largest clean-energy exhibitions, with 1,000+ brands across 14 halls and more than 55,000 visitors (organiser's own figures).

## What's on show

- **Solar:** modules, inverters, mounting and rooftop systems.
- **Energy storage:** batteries and grid-scale storage.
- **Green hydrogen** and bio-energy.
- **E-mobility:** charging infrastructure and EV components.

If you're attending, check visitor registration on the official site. Registration deadlines can fall before the show.

## Why I'm writing about a renewable energy expo

Because I build websites for clean-energy businesses. One example is [Saurally](/projects/saurally), a solar e-commerce store with 40+ products, filtering, comparison and multi-gateway payments. I see the same gap again and again: excellent products, and websites that don't do them justice.

## What a solar or clean-energy business website needs

1. **Product pages for engineers and homeowners alike:** specifications and datasheets for installers, and plain-language benefits and savings for homeowners.
2. **A quote or site-survey flow**, not just a contact form: roof size, location, monthly bill and connection type.
3. **Subsidy and finance information**, stated clearly and kept up to date.
4. **Local SEO** for "solar installer in [city]" searches, with service-area pages that are genuinely useful.
5. **Trust:** certifications, completed installations with photos, and reviews.

## After the expo

The leads you collect at a trade show go cold quickly. A website with a strong quote flow and fast follow-up turns expo conversations into orders.
