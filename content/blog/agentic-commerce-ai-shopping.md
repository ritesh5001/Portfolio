**Agentic commerce means AI agents doing the shopping: a customer says "find me a cotton kurta under ₹1,500 that arrives by Friday" and an agent searches stores, compares options and, with permission, completes the checkout.** It's early, but the infrastructure is being built right now. In October 2026, Sierra and Meta announced the Personal Agent Protocol with Walmart, Shopify and Stripe as founding partners, alongside standards from Google and Visa.

## How it changes the buying journey

Today a shopper visits five sites. With an agent, the agent visits them, and the shopper may never see your homepage. Agents decide based on:

- **Data they can read:** price, stock, delivery date, return policy and specifications.
- **Trust signals:** reviews, clear policies, and a business the agent can verify.
- **A checkout they can complete**, via emerging protocols, with the customer's approval.

Your beautiful banner doesn't help with any of that. Your product data does.

## The standards forming

- **Personal Agent Protocol (Sierra and Meta):** how an agent signs in to a business and acts for a customer. Specification due in October 2026.
- **Universal Commerce Protocol (co-developed by Google)** and **Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol** cover overlapping ground.
- **Payment providers like Stripe** are building agent payment flows.

Expect consolidation. You don't need to pick a winner today.

## How an Indian store prepares

1. **Complete product data**: sizes, materials, colours, real stock, and delivery estimates by pincode.
2. **Product structured data** on every product page.
3. **Policies in text**: returns, COD and shipping, not inside images.
4. **A fast, server-rendered store**, which agents and Google can both read.
5. **A platform that will support agent protocols.** Shopify is a founding partner, and WooCommerce plugins and custom integrations will follow.
