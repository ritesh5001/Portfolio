**Microsoft Ignite 2026 takes place November 17–20 at the Moscone Center in San Francisco.** It's Microsoft's biggest annual event for IT, developers and business customers. Based on the last few years, expect the headlines to centre on Copilot, AI agents, Azure AI and security. Check Microsoft's official Ignite site for the confirmed agenda.

## What to watch

- **Copilot across Microsoft 365:** more agent-like features that take actions across Outlook, Teams, Word and Excel, not just answer questions.
- **Agents for business:** tools for building and governing AI agents connected to company data, plus controls on what they're allowed to do.
- **Azure AI:** model availability (Microsoft's own and partners'), pricing, and infrastructure for running AI at scale.
- **Security and governance:** identity, data protection and AI security controls, increasingly important as agents get access to business systems. See [MCP security](/blog/mcp-security-adoption).
- **Developer tools:** GitHub Copilot and coding agents, following [GitHub Universe](/blog/github-universe-2026-ai-agents) in October.

## What matters for small and mid-sized businesses

Big-event announcements often target large enterprises. The useful questions for a smaller business are:

1. **Does it work with what you already pay for?** Many Copilot features come with Microsoft 365 business plans.
2. **Where does your data go?** Check admin controls before turning AI on company-wide.
3. **What does it cost per user, really,** once you add the AI tiers?
4. **Does it connect to your actual business systems,** like your store, CRM or ERP? That's usually where custom integration comes in.
